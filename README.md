## About the app

Oxford Vale is a full-stack e-commerce website with a storefront for customers and a private admin panel for running the business. It delivers across the UK and worldwide, with prices in US dollars and payment by card, Apple Pay, Google Pay, Klarna or PayPal.

## For customers

- **Browse and search:** category and collection pages with filters for size, colour and price, plus search with live suggestions.
- **Product pages:** image gallery, size and colour selection, live stock status, fabric and care notes, and related products.
- **Cart and checkout:** a cart that survives refreshes, guest checkout, coupons, and delivery options by country.
- **Orders:** order confirmation, email updates, and tracking by order number.
- **Accounts:** order history and saved addresses.
- **Any device:** an app-style layout with bottom navigation on phones and tablets, and light and dark mode throughout.

## For the store team

- **Dashboard:** sales, top products, orders to act on, low stock and open carts.
- **Orders:** status updates, courier and tracking details, PDF invoices and CSV export.
- **Catalogue:** products, photos, sizes, stock, categories, collections, coupons and delivery options.
- **Customers:** customer records, cart leads, abandoned-cart alerts and contact messages.
- **Team access:** Owner, Manager and Staff roles, with an audit log of every admin change.
